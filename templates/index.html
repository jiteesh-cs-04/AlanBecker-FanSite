Who is Alan Becker?

Alan Becker is an American animator and YouTuber from Ohio. In June 2006, at 17, he posted the first Animator vs. Animation, a Flash cartoon where a stick figure fights back against the cursor of the animator who drew him. It took off, and he has been building on that idea ever since.

That idea grew into a whole universe of "Animation vs." videos: stick figures moving through the worlds of games and software, and later even school subjects.
